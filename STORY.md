# ZİRVE GECESİ (Gece 1/5)

Psychological "would you open the door?" reel, built on the storytelling pattern of
@farazisenaryo "Fener Gecesi" (instagram.com/reel/De27zszoLYg, 81.7 s, 19.9K likes, 655 comments).

Craft: flat 2D vector art, navy night, captions 1-3 words at a time (Oswald, white with amber/red key word),
persistent HUD (GECE 1/7, live clock, YARDIM 7 GÜN), slow push-ins, a cut every 2-4 s, calm male TTS
(~2.3 words/s), no silence under narration (ambience bed), red only for danger words.

## Our version: what changes and what stays

Same: 2nd person, isolated workplace at night, omen, authority broadcast with 3 rules, resource vs deadline,
time jump, impersonated rescuers with a countdown, your-own-handwriting twist, whisper ending, AÇ/AÇMA CTA,
"Gece 1/N" series card.

Changed identity: snowed-in mountain weather station on Erciyes instead of a sea lighthouse; ice-blue/slate
palette with a warm orange lamp accent (no navy/red-yellow); orange parka character with beanie; falling snow,
film grain and vignette on every shot; Bebas Neue captions with an underline bar; HUD shows temperature and
"YOL KAPALI"; the broadcast is a teletype radio card, rules are taped index cards.

New psychological engine (doubt about yourself, not only the monster):
1. Rule 2 "if you hear your own voice on the radio, don't answer" is set up, then your exact report comes back
   over the radio although you never pressed the button.
2. Footprints lead to the door, none lead away.
3. The logbook has six reports tonight; you wrote one. The last line, in your handwriting, is timestamped one
   minute in the future: "03:24. Kapıyı açtım."
4. The voice at the door: "İçerideki sen değilsin. Asıl nöbetçi benim." Your reflection in the window smiles
   while you don't. The viewer has to decide who is real, which drives AÇ / AÇMA comments.
